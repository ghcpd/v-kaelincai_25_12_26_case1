## Critical Implementation Notes

### The LR Rotation Fix
- **Buggy code**: `right_rotate(node)` first, then `left_rotate(node.left)`
- **Fixed code**: `left_rotate(node.left)` first, then `right_rotate(node)`
- **Rationale**: LR is a double-rotation requiring two steps:
  1. Left-rotate the left child (straighten zig-zag into left-left pattern)
  2. Right-rotate the parent (rebalance the tree)
- Reversing this sequence corrupts links and makes nodes unreachable
